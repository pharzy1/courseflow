# Recruiter brief

## One-line summary

CourseFlow is a production-style Berkeley course-intelligence platform that combines a 5,900+ section data pipeline, explainable constraint-based schedule optimization, authenticated cross-device plans, enrollment monitoring, and observable source quality.

## Résumé bullets

- Built and deployed a TypeScript/React scheduling platform that enumerates real section combinations, rejects time conflicts and hard constraints, and ranks feasible schedules with a fully reconciled preference score.
- Designed a hybrid data layer for 5,900+ Berkeley sections with official-metadata overlays, Neon persistence, record-level provenance, freshness gates, enrollment history, and transparent transitional-source disclosure.
- Implemented Clerk-isolated cloud plans, shareable schedules, recurring iCalendar export, Resend notification safeguards, structured telemetry, and automated unit, integration, accessibility, and three-viewport browser testing.

Do not claim affiliation with UC Berkeley, CalCentral integration, an official degree audit, official section/grade data while transitional adapters remain active, real-user adoption before the study is completed, or performance figures that have not been measured in production.
