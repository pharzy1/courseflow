# Berkeley student validation kit

## Recruitment target

Recruit 12 current Berkeley students: four actively planning next term, four transfer or double-major students, two students using assistive technology, and two students with jobs, athletics, or commute constraints. Do not collect student IDs, grades, disability diagnoses, or CalCentral credentials.

## 20-minute protocol

1. Find two courses they may take and explain which data they trust.
2. Add alternative sections and create one hard constraint plus two preferences.
3. Generate options, explain why option one won, and identify any concern.
4. Share the plan, export it, save it, reload, and restore it.
5. Rate task confidence and usefulness from 1–5.

Record completion, time-on-task, errors, abandoned steps, System Usability Scale responses, and one verbatim improvement request. Success gates: ≥80% unassisted completion, median ≤7 minutes, score explanation understood by ≥10/12, and no accessibility blocker. Obtain consent before recording; otherwise use anonymous notes.
